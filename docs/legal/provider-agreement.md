# Pillow Thief Provider Agreement

**Last updated:** [DATE]
**Between:** [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("Pillow Thief") and the provider ("you").

## 1. Independent contractor

You provide your services as an independent contractor. Nothing in this
agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership or joint-venture
relationship. You control how you deliver your services, when you are available,
and what you charge (within platform limits). You are responsible for your own
taxes, registrations, insurance and legal compliance.

## 2. Eligibility and verification

You must be at least 18 years old. You agree to provide accurate legal name,
date of birth, identity documentation, and payout details, and to keep them
current. Pillow Thief may verify this information and may decline, suspend or
remove a provider at any time where verification fails or where safety,
accuracy, or policy concerns arise. Your legal name, identity documents and
financial information are stored privately and are never shown publicly.

## 3. Your profile and services

- You must describe your services accurately, including what a session involves
  and what it does not.
- All services are **non-sexual**. Sexual services, sexual solicitation, and any
  implication of sexual services are prohibited and are grounds for immediate
  termination.
- You must state your prices, session durations, meeting options, travel zones
  and boundaries clearly, and honour confirmed bookings as agreed.
- You must keep your availability accurate and block time you cannot work.

## 4. Client conduct and safety

- You may decline any booking request.
- You may pause or end any session at any time if you feel unsafe, uncomfortable
  or disrespected — with no penalty from the platform.
- You must not retaliate against a client for reporting a concern, declining
  contact, or ending a session.
- Report safety incidents through the app promptly so we can act.

## 5. Pricing, fees and payouts

- You set your prices. The platform adds a booking fee where applicable and
  retains commission of [15]% of the booking value (excluding tax), as shown in
  your earnings dashboard. We will give [30] days' notice before changing the
  commission rate.
- Travel fees, when offered, are calculated by the platform from road-distance
  estimates and your configured service-area rates.
- Payments collected from clients are recorded against your account. Payouts are
  made [weekly/fortnightly] to your registered payout destination, less
  commission and any refunds, chargebacks or adjustments, with a transaction
  record for each payout.
- You are responsible for any tax obligations arising from your earnings.

## 6. Cancellations and refunds

Client cancellations follow the cancellation policy displayed at booking
(platform default: full refund ≥ 24 hours, 50% between 6 and 24 hours, no refund
under 6 hours). If you decline a request or cancel a confirmed booking, the
client is refunded in full, and repeated provider-initiated cancellations may
affect your standing on the platform.

## 7. Confidentiality and privacy

- You will receive client information only as needed to deliver a confirmed
  booking. You must not use it for any other purpose, and must not share exact
  addresses, contact details or messages with anyone else.
- Do not record or photograph a session without the other person's explicit
  consent, and never publish anything that identifies a client.
- Messaging inside the platform is the supported channel; private contact
  details should not be solicited or shared.

## 8. Prohibited conduct

Sexual solicitation or services; harassment, threats, discrimination or
stalking; coercion of any kind; fraud or misrepresentation of identity,
qualifications or services; recording without consent; sharing private
information; providing services while intoxicated or under the influence;
soliciting clients to transact off-platform to avoid fees.

## 9. Suspension and termination

Either party may end this agreement at any time. Pillow Thief may suspend or
terminate immediately for safety incidents, policy breaches, suspected
prohibited services, fraudulent activity, or legal requirements. Outstanding
amounts legitimately earned before termination are settled, subject to refunds,
chargebacks and investigations.

## 10. Liability and insurance

You are responsible for your own conduct and the services you provide. You agree
to maintain any insurance required by law or reasonably appropriate for your
services. Pillow Thief does not control, direct or supervise your sessions.
Nothing in this agreement excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 11. Dispute resolution

Contact [providers@yourdomain] first. Unresolved disputes are handled under the
governing law of Botswana, with the courts of Botswana having jurisdiction.

## 12. Acceptance

By applying to become a provider and accepting these terms in the app, you
confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to this agreement, and that
the information you supplied during verification is accurate.
