## 1. Who we are

Pillow Thief is a booking platform for non-sexual professional cuddling and
companionship services, initially operating in Gaborone, Botswana. We connect
clients with independent, verified providers.

We are the data controller for account, booking, payment-record and moderation
data processed through the platform. Providers act as independent data
controllers for the personal information they receive as part of a confirmed
booking.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information:** display name, email address, password (stored as a
cryptographic hash by our authentication provider — we never see your password),
preferred language, country, notification preferences, and timestamps of your
acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy and your 18+ confirmation.

**Provider application and verification:** legal name, date of birth,
identity-document images, identity document type, payout destination reference
issued by our payment provider, and verification decisions with reviewer notes.
This data is stored separately from your public profile and is never shown to
other users.

**Booking information:** services booked, session duration, scheduled time
(stored in UTC, displayed in Africa/Gaborone), meeting arrangement, the
meeting point you select (address label and coordinates), prices, fees, taxes,
commission, payment status and booking status history.

**Location information:** if you grant permission, your device location is used
to centre the map and to find nearby providers. Location is requested only when
you actively use a location feature; there is **no background location
tracking**. You can search manually without granting permission.

**Communications:** messages you exchange through the platform, reviews you
write, reports and support tickets you submit, and safety notes you add to a
booking (for example a consent checklist).

**Payment information:** transaction identifiers, amounts, status, refunds and
payout records. Card numbers and security codes are processed by our payment
provider and **never** reach or are stored on our systems.

**Technical information:** session tokens, approximate device/OS version, and
security-relevant events (for example failed sign-in attempts). We keep an
audit log of administrative actions.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to
train advertising profiles.

## 4. Who we share information with

- **The other booking participant.** Only what is needed for the session: the
  counterparty's display name, profile, agreed meeting point for that booking,
  and messages exchanged. Providers do not receive your email address or phone
  number through the platform.
- **Payment providers.** To process payments, refunds and payouts.
- **Google Maps Platform.** Map display, address lookup, and distance estimation
  (see section 7).
- **Messaging/push infrastructure.** For in-app and push notifications.
- **Professional advisers, auditors, insurers and authorities** where required
  by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not publish your exact address, identity documents, payment details, or
private contact information.

## 5. Location privacy specifics

- Providers publish approximate locations and service areas only. Exact home
  coordinates are never exposed through the platform.
- Exact meeting points for a booking are visible only to the two participants
  and to authorised administrators investigating a safety or payment issue.
- Clients' home addresses are never shown to other users.
- We request foreground location permission only when you activate a feature
  that needs it, and we do not retain a location history.

## 6. Retention

| Data | Retention |

## 7. Third-party services and international transfers

We use Google Maps Platform (maps, places, routing). Google may process request
data under its own terms; we do not send Google your name, email address or
identity documents. Some service providers process data outside Botswana. Where
that happens we rely on the provider's contractual safeguards and we minimise
what is transferred.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your data, correction of
inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to processing,
and portability. You can exercise deletion and correction directly in the app
(Settings → Delete my account; profile editor). For other requests, contact
[privacy@yourdomain]. You may also complain to the relevant Botswana
authority.

Deleting your account: we deactivate and unpublish your profile immediately,
cancel upcoming bookings, anonymise your display name, and delete your
authentication record. Financial and safety records are retained as required by
section 6.

## 9. Security

We use row-level security on every database table, encrypted transport (TLS),
a private storage bucket for identity documents, least-privilege credentials,
signature-verified payment webhooks, and append-only audit logging for
administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong,
unique password and report suspected compromises immediately.

## 10. Children

Pillow Thief is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly
collect data from minors. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we
will close it and delete the associated data.

## 11. Changes

We will notify you of material changes before they take effect and update the
"Last updated" date above.
